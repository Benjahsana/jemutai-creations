Jemutai, in her words

How did you start?

My mom taught me to crochet. After that I kept learning on my own, from YouTube and from trying things, until I could sew and crochet much better.

What piece are you proudest of?

My crochet wedding dress. I made it on a tight deadline, using only the yarn I already had.

What do you want to make in the US?

Anything that makes someone feel good in their own body. I love it when a piece fits someone's shape perfectly.

What do you love making most?

Dresses and skirts. Give me fabric and a dress idea and I'm happy all day.

Who do you make for?

Everyone: women, men, children. If you can wear it, I can make it.

What sizes?

Your size. Nothing comes off a rack. You send me your measurements and I make it to fit you.

Favorite colors?

White, black and red. Those are my colors.

What should people know about you?

I don't like to disappoint anyone. When you ask me for something, I make it well and I make it on time. What you want is what you'll get.
